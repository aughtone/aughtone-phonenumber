# Research

Investigations: the question that came up, the options weighed, the dead ends walked far enough to rule out, what was found, and what to do about it. A design still being worked out lives here for as long as it is moving.

**Not here:** the settled rules that came out of an investigation, once they have hardened — those become a [Decision](../decisions/README.md), and this record links to them.

The records below explore the three libphonenumber subsystems [ADR-0001](../decisions/ADR-0001-scope-of-the-libphonenumber-port.md) placed out of scope. None has a decision yet; each frames what porting it would cost, so a future "should we?" starts from evidence rather than from zero.
